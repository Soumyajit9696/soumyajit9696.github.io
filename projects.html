Research Reports

M.Sc. Project NIT Silchar · Dept. of Physics · 2026

Ab-initio DFT study of monolayer & bilayer ZrS2 and ZrSe2

My M.Sc. dissertation: a density-functional theory study of the two-dimensional transition-metal dichalcogenides ZrS2 and ZrSe2 — from relaxed crystal structures through to band structures and density of states. The through-line is the monolayer–bilayer comparison: what a second layer, and the van der Waals gap it opens between the two, does to a 2D material's electronic structure.

Structural relaxation · Band structure · Density of states · Monolayer vs bilayer · Interlayer vdW gap · 2D TMDs

VASP · Ab-initio DFT · 2D materials

Summer Internship IISER Kolkata · Dept. of Physical Sciences · 2025

First-Principles DFT Calculations on Crystalline Materials

The report of my summer at the Department of Physical Sciences, IISER Kolkata (May–July 2025): first-principles calculations on crystalline materials, worked through two codes — plane-wave VASP and the all-electron Elk. Total energies, relaxed geometries, band structures and density of states: the full pipeline from a raw crystal structure to physics you can plot.

Total energies · Relaxed geometries · Band structure · Density of states · Two-code workflow

VASP · Elk · First-principles DFT · Summer 2025

VaspViz

VASP post-processing & visualization suite

Flagship

A cross-platform desktop application I built with PyQt6 for analyzing and visualizing VASP (Vienna Ab initio Simulation Package) output. VaspViz turns raw first-principles data into publication-ready figures — band structures, density of states, optical spectra and more — without writing a single line of script. What used to be an afternoon of matplotlib plumbing becomes a few clicks.

Band structure · DOS · Fat bands · Wannier90 · Optical spectra · 3D POSCAR viewer · CHGCAR rendering · EOS fitting · Convergence monitor

Python · PyQt6 · Matplotlib · NumPy · SciPy · pyqtgraph · AI-assisted
