Physics, code, and the physics of materials

I study how matter behaves one atom at a time — and I build the software that makes that research faster for everyone.

01

The short version

Hello! I'm Soumyajit, a physics postgraduate who codes

I'm a Physics postgraduate at the National Institute of Technology Silchar, specialising in computational condensed matter physics. My current research uses density functional theory to study two-dimensional materials — probing their band structures, optical response and potential for optoelectronic applications.

Beyond research, I'm an active scientific software developer. I created VaspViz, a PyQt6 desktop suite for post-processing VASP calculations, along with several smaller tools — because I believe good software should shave hours off a researcher's week.

When I'm not parsing vasprun.xml files, you'll find me exploring Linux, writing about science on my blog, or following football. The fastest way to reach me is dsoumyajit295@gmail.com.
